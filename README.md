 📊 Quantum Electronics – Sales & Inventory Performance Dashboard

📌 Project Overview

The **Quantum Electronics Dashboard** is a Business Intelligence project designed to analyze **sales performance, revenue, profitability, product categories, store performance, and time-based sales trends**.

The dashboard provides an interactive overview of business performance through three main analytical pages:

- **Overview & KPIs**
- **Store Performance**
- **Products & Time Trends**

The objective of this project is to transform business data into meaningful insights that can support **data-driven decision-making and performance improvement**.

---
🎯 Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project are to:

- Analyze overall revenue and profitability.
- Evaluate performance across different product categories.
- Identify the highest and lowest-performing stores.
- Analyze product profitability.
- Identify revenue patterns across different days of the week.
- Analyze monthly and yearly revenue trends.
- Identify seasonal performance patterns.
- Provide actionable business recommendations based on the analysis.

💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis:

 1. Focus on High-Margin Categories

Increase marketing focus on the **Electronics** category because of its high **45% profit margin**.

 2. Improve Toys Profitability

Although **Toys** generates the highest revenue, its profit margin is relatively low at **21%**.

 3. Investigate Underperforming Stores

Analyze the lowest-performing stores based on factors such as:

- Location
- Inventory
- Pricing
- Operational efficiency

 4. Optimize Friday & Saturday Sales

Increase promotional activities, product availability, and inventory levels during the highest-revenue days.

 5. Prepare for Seasonal Declines

Plan ahead for the recurring revenue decline during **July and August** through seasonal promotions and targeted campaigns.

 6. Expand High-Margin Products

Give additional attention to products such as **Jenga, Mini Basketball, and Playfoam**, particularly in high-performing stores.
